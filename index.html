Vocal Health Indicators
These metrics are early-warning signals. Healthy values appear in teal; values outside normal range appear in amber or rose.
HNR
–
Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio — breathiness indicator. Healthy singing: >20 dB. Low values suggest breathy or pressed phonation.
Jitter
–
Cycle-to-cycle pitch variation. Normal: <1%. Elevated jitter is an early sign of vocal fatigue or strain.
Shimmer
–
Cycle-to-cycle amplitude variation. Normal: <3 dB. Elevated shimmer can indicate laryngeal tension or fatigue.
Spectral Tilt
–
Energy roll-off with frequency. A flatter tilt = brighter, more forward placement. Steep tilt = darker or covered tone.
Health Over Session
HNR (teal) and jitter (amber) over time. Downward trends may indicate fatigue.
